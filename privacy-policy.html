Privacy Policy of Naman Construction Solutions

At Naman Construction Solutions, accessible from https://namanconstructionsolutions.in/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Naman Construction Solutions and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry form or book an appointment on our website, we may ask you for personal contact information including your Name, Company Name, Address, Email Address, and Telephone Number. This information is solely used to communicate with you regarding your construction inquiries, site visits, and project estimates.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our engineering and construction services.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our design and contracting offerings.
  • Communicate with you for customer service, site scheduling, updates, and estimation reports.
  • Process municipal plan sanction filings and statutory clearance documents on your behalf.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities and ensure job site safety compliance.

Blueprint & Intellectual Property Protection

All architectural drawings, 3D visualizations, structural calculations, and BOQ estimates prepared by Naman Construction Solutions for our clients are kept strictly confidential and will never be sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.
