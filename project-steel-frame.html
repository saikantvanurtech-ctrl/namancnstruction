Steel Frame

Home / Project / Steel Frame

Description

The Steel Frame project by Naman Construction Solutions is part of our civil engineering and building delivery work across Western Odisha. Structural steel framing and connection detailing erected to the engineer's drawings alongside RCC work.

Every stage is executed by our in-house team with IS-certified materials, documented quality checks and clear communication with the client from planning to handover.

Construction Materials

Every phase of construction incorporated high-performance materials tested for longevity, environmental efficiency, and fire safety:

Traditional Materials Grade 53/43 cement, washed sand, burnt clay bricks & calibrated machine aggregate
Alternate Building Materials Cellular lightweight concrete (CLC) blocks & low-emissivity double glazed windows
Composite Materials Glass fiber reinforced concrete (GFRC) ornamental panels & structural epoxy grouts
Smart Materials Anti-carbonation protective coatings & phase-change interior insulation boards
