Common Questions About Our Services

Everything you need to know about starting your building project in Bargarh, understanding our cost structures, and tracking construction progress.

What services does Naman Construction Solutions provide?
We provide full-lifecycle building and architectural services including Turnkey Building Construction, Renovation & Remodeling, 3D Architectural Planning, Elevation Design, Detailed BOQ Cost Estimation, Vastu Shastra Consultation, Municipal Building Plan Approval, and Luxury Interior Design.
How do you calculate the cost of construction?
We develop a comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ) based on your architectural drawings. We calculate exact quantities of steel (TMT 550D), cement bags, brick counts, sand, gravel, and labor rates. This provides you with an itemized budget with stage-wise milestone payments and zero hidden surprise charges.
Do you help with municipal building approvals in Odisha?
Yes! Our certified structural engineers (Er. Satyanarayan Bhusagar) prepare sanction drawings strictly conforming to the Odisha Development Authorities (ODA) rules and National Building Code. We coordinate portal submissions, structural safety certificates, and fire/utility NOCs to ensure your permit is cleared promptly.
Can I customize the 3D plan and elevation before starting work?
Absolutely. Our 3D design studio generates photorealistic computer visualizations of your floor plans and external elevations. We iterate through multiple design revisions with you until every room layout, window placement, and facade material meets your complete satisfaction.
How long does it take to construct an independent home?
A standard 2,000 to 3,500 sq. ft. residential duplex generally takes 6 to 9 months from foundation excavation to final painting and electrical handover. We provide a milestone-tracked project schedule and assign dedicated on-site supervisors to ensure zero avoidable delays.
How do you incorporate Vastu Shastra principles?
We map the 16 Vastu energy zones on your plot layout before finalizing structural blueprints. We ensure the main entrance aligns with Ishanya (North-East), the kitchen with Agneya (South-East), and master bedroom with Nairutya (South-West) to create directional harmony without compromising modern design.
How can I book an initial consultation or site visit?
You can use our online Book Appointment wizard, call our office directly at +91 9853953883, or visit our office located at Panchayat College Road, Near Kalimandir Chowk, Bargarh. Initial plot consultations in Bargarh are conducted free of charge.
